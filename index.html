About me

I'm a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from BRAC University with hands-on experience in data analysis, web application development and workflow automation. I enjoy taking messy, scattered data and turning it into clear answers that teams can act on.

Currently I work as a Junior Data Analyst at CarryBee, where I build ETL and reporting workflows with Python, PostgreSQL, n8n and Google Apps Script, analyze merchant churn and delivery performance, and develop KPI dashboards in Metabase and Power BI.

My interests lie in AI-powered automation, business analysis and data engineering: understanding how a business runs, measuring it with the right KPIs, and building the pipelines, AI agents, dashboards and integrated applications that streamline operations and support data-driven decisions.
